### New

- The Mac window keeps its destinations in a tab bar across the top instead of a sidebar, so the
  full width of the window goes to the artwork.
- My List moved to the profile tab, and it now syncs with your Nuvio account — a title saved on
  the television is in the list on the Mac, and the other way round.
- Hovering a poster turns it over to the backdrop, with a line about what the title is, and the
  posters beside it stand aside to make room.

### Bug fixes

- Scrolling through a hub no longer stalls the app.
- Sources that a host was too busy to answer for are no longer written off as dead. NuviOS now
  tries a working one rather than stopping at a list of timed-out links, and asks each source
  once rather than several times over.
- The player owns the whole window again: neither the source picker nor the tab bar sits on top
  of the picture.
- Playback speed follows what you chose. It could get stuck reading 2× while playing at normal
  speed after a press-and-hold.
- A film is no longer labelled LIVE while it works out how long it is.
