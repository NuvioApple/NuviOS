### New

- A title's cast now shows real photos, not just initials. A TMDB key (in
  Settings, or baked in by whoever built this copy) gives an exact match;
  without one, a Wikipedia lookup fills in what it can.
- A series' episodes now sit right on its detail page, with a season picker
  above them, instead of behind a separate list.
- Pressing a tab you're already on now takes you back to that tab's top
  level, rather than sitting there doing nothing while a folder or detail
  page stays open on top of it.
- A folder now has its own Movies / Shows / All filter, so a mixed catalog
  can be narrowed without leaving the folder.

### Bug fixes

- Streaming-service marketing tiles ("Netflix Top 10", "Apple TV+ Top 10", …)
  no longer show up as folders with nothing playable behind them.
- A catalog whose genre comes back as the literal word "None" no longer shows
  "None" as if it were a real genre.
