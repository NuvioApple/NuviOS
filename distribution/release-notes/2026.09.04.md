### New

- A profile can now use any picture on the web. Edit a profile and paste a link
  into **Custom image URL**, and that picture becomes the avatar on every device
  signed into your account.
- **Browse avatars** opens a browser right in the profile editor, starting at
  the Nuvio avatar gallery. Tap a picture and it becomes your avatar — no
  copying links by hand. It works on any site, not just the gallery.
- Every profile in the switcher now has an edit button, so you can change a
  profile without switching to it first.

### Bug fixes

- Choosing a new avatar now actually changes it. A profile that had a custom
  picture set kept showing the old one no matter what you picked, because the
  saved link always won over the new choice.
