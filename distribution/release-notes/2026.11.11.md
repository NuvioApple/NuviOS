### New

- The Home hero is now picked for you. It shows newer films and shows similar
  to what you're at least 40% through, instead of whatever is popular. Drag
  across it, or swipe with two fingers on a trackpad, to move to the next one.
- Remove a title from Continue Watching by right-clicking its card, or with
  Clear Progress on the film's page. It's removed from your account's watch
  history too, so it won't come back from another device.
- The "Who's watching?" screen has bigger profile pictures.

### Better

- Trailers before a movie no longer buffer. Each one downloads in full before
  it plays, while the next one downloads in the background.
- Restart now really starts the movie from the beginning, and plays the
  trailers first.
- Leaving a movie takes you back to its page rather than the list of sources.
- Anything you've started shows on Continue Watching, even if you only watched
  a minute or two.
