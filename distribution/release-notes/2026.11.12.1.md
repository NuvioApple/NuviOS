### New

- The player can go full screen. Click the full-screen button at the top
  right, press F, or double-click the picture. Esc takes you back to a window,
  and closing the player does too.

### Changed

- The zoom button in the player is gone; full screen takes its place. F now
  goes full screen instead of switching between fit and fill.
- Double-clicking the picture goes full screen instead of skipping 10
  seconds. The arrow keys and J and L still skip.
