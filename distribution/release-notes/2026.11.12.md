### New

- Six cinema intros are built in: IMAX Countdown, IMAX Never Compromise,
  IMAX Pre-Show, Dolby Cinema, Dolby Universe and Infinity Vision. Turn on
  "Play an intro before movies" in the profile tab, then choose which ones
  can play. Your own intro addresses still work alongside them.
- Intros take turns. Each one you've switched on plays once, in random order,
  before any of them comes round again, so you won't see the same one twice
  in a row.

### Changed

- The player no longer shows chapters: the Chapters menu, the chapter name
  under the title, the marks on the time bar and the previous and next chapter
  buttons are gone. The previous and next track keys on a keyboard or remote
  now skip back and forward 10 seconds instead.

This update is larger than usual, about 590 MB, because the intros are
included in the app.
